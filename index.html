🎯 1. What This Script Solves

Most traders lose money not because they can't find charts, but because of poor regime awareness, chasing extended breakouts, and arbitrary sizing.

  • Filters 500 noisy stocks down to 5-10 high-probability setups.
  • Checks market regime first: if Nifty < 200 SMA, it warns you to stay in cash.
  • Derives stop-loss and share quantity directly from volatility (ATR) rather than feelings.

🔬 2. The 8 Screening Rules Decoded

  • Turnover ≥ ₹5 Cr: Filters illiquid operators and circuit-locked penny traps.
  • P > SMA50 > SMA200: Classic Stan Weinstein Stage 2 / Mark Minervini trend template.
  • ≤ 6% from 60d High: Catches stocks coiling near all-time / multi-month highs.
  • ≤ 8% above SMA20: Prevents chasing overextended climaxes.
  • ATR 1.5% - 6.0%: Avoids dead stocks and ultra-wild lottery tickets.

⚠️ 3. Strengths vs Failure Points

Huge Strengths: Small account awareness (`_unaffordable` check), Zerodha fee estimation, and no future-price prediction illusions.

Real-world Weaknesses:

  • archives.nseindia.com blocks requests without custom browser headers.
  • yfinance batching can face IP rate-limiting in automated crons.
  • Ignores earnings calendar / quarterly result date blackouts.
